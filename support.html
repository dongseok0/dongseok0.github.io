LoopTube Support

LoopTube is an iOS app for creating precise A-B loops on YouTube videos and saving them as reusable presets.

FAQ

How do I set A/B loop points?

Open a YouTube video in LoopTube, move to the point where you want the loop to start, and tap the A button. Then move to the point where you want the loop to end and tap the B button. Enable the Loop toggle — playback will repeat between those two points.

How do I save a loop preset?

After setting your A and B points, tap the save (heart) button and enter a title. The preset is stored locally on your device and appears in the Saved tab. Tap it any time to reopen that loop instantly.

How do I share a loop with someone?

In the Saved tab, tap the share button on any preset. This generates a link that opens LoopTube directly at that video with the A/B points pre-loaded. If the recipient has LoopTube installed, tapping the link opens the loop immediately.

How do I delete a saved preset?

In the Saved tab, swipe left on the preset you want to remove and tap Delete. The preset is removed from your device.
