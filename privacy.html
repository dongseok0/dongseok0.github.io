--- --- LoopTube Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-13

This Privacy Policy applies to LoopTube, an iOS app published under the bundle ID com.perkmine.LoopTube. LoopTube is a YouTube A-B loop player that lets you set loop ranges and save presets on your device.

Summary: LoopTube does not require an account, does not require sign-in, and does not send your saved loop data to developer-operated servers.

Information Stored by the App

LoopTube stores your saved loop preset data locally on your device using SwiftData.

The locally stored data may include:

  • Loop preset title
  • YouTube video ID
  • Loop start time
  • Loop end time

This data stays on your device and is never sent to developer servers.

No Account or Sign-In Required

LoopTube does not provide developer account registration, user login, or sign-in features. You can use the app without creating an account.

YouTube Embedded Player

LoopTube uses the YouTube embedded player to play YouTube videos inside the app. When you use this functionality, YouTube and Google may collect data according to their own policies and terms.

Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

LoopTube does not control the data practices of YouTube or Google.

How Your Data Is Used

The locally stored preset data is used only to provide app features such as saving, reopening, and managing your A-B loop presets on your device.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored preset data with third parties. LoopTube does not transmit your saved preset information to developer-operated servers.

Data Deletion

Because LoopTube stores its preset data locally on your device, you can delete that data by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

LoopTube does not knowingly collect personal information directly from users through a developer-operated account system or backend service. Use of YouTube content remains subject to YouTube and Google policies.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer at perkmine@gmail.com.
